# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Command center operators, emergency responders, and system administrators who monitor live accidents, dispatch units, and analyze system health. They operate under high pressure and need clarity, speed, and accuracy.

## Product Purpose
An incident command center for an embedded accident detection system with sensor fusion. It allows operators to view live incidents, track units, communicate via SOS, and manage alerts. Success looks like reducing response times to critical emergencies while keeping operators focused and calm.

## Brand Personality
Warm, editorial, calm, and authoritative. It draws from the cream + coral aesthetic (reminiscent of Claude.com) rather than generic dark-mode SaaS. It must feel premium, highly crafted, and deeply intentional.

## Anti-references
- AI slop, overused glassmorphism, or generic gradient text.
- Cluttered, information-dense SaaS dashboards that rely on repeating ghost-cards or tiny uppercase kickers.
- Generic tech blues and grays; it must retain its warm editorial identity.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over density:** Provide information progressively rather than cramming everything into one view. Space is a tool for focus, but shouldn't be wasted.
- **Editorial authority:** Use typography and careful color strategy to guide the eye instead of boxes and borders.
- **Calm urgency:** It is an emergency system, but the interface must project calm control, using color (coral/red) deliberately for active incidents only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for legibility in varied lighting conditions (e.g., bright command centers or mobile responder units).
- Clear typography for fast reading; no cramped letter-spacing.
- Color alone must not convey state (use icons and text labels).
